Your Information

This page explains how The Blessing Box uses information you submit through its website forms. Christmas wishes, Blessing Box Experience requests, and reader feedback are collected through Netlify Forms. Contact messages are sent by email to the recipient you choose.

Christmas wishes

The Christmas wish form asks for a child’s first name, what they wish for, what they would give, and an adult’s email address and permission. The wish goes to Santa through The Blessing Box website, and a copy is sent to the adult email address provided.

Blessing Box Experience requests

We collect the requested quantity and the adult’s name, email address, mailing address, and phone number when you ask for a Blessing Box Experience. We use those details to respond to the request and coordinate delivery.

Contact messages

We use your optional name, email address, selected recipient and topic, and message to deliver your inquiry to Liv or Quincy so they can respond. Please avoid including sensitive personal information.

Reader feedback

We collect your first name or pen name, optional city and state, optional email address, review, and permission choice so Liv can review your feedback and respond if needed. Reviews are handled privately before any publication. With your permission, Liv may quote your review using the name you provide. Email addresses are kept out of published reviews. Submitting feedback does not sign you up for release notifications.

For adult visitors

Parents, guardians, educators, and other adult readers should complete these forms. Children should ask an adult to get in touch on their behalf.

Questions or removal requests

To ask about your information or request removal of your feedback or a published quote, email livfullerauthor@gmail.com.
