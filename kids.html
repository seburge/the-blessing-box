Meet the Book

The Blessing Box

By Liv Fuller

Illustrated by Quincy Battieste

All Hugh wants for Christmas is a dog, but an out-of-sorts Santa has something else in mind—an idea his elves think is nuttier than a fruitcake.

When Hugh opens Santa’s unusual gift, the Blessing Box, his dream of a dog of his own crumbles like a Christmas cookie. But Hugh soon realizes the mystical box isn’t empty after all. It holds an invisible key called opportunity—one that could help him prove to his parents he’s ready for a dog.

From one Christmas to the next, Hugh, a sixth grader on the autism spectrum, works tirelessly to make his dream come true. But bullies, betrayal, and the challenge of standing up for himself may stand in the way.

Fortunately, Hugh’s mischievous best friend is determined to help—even if she lands in the doghouse trying. And if one extraordinary elf decides Hugh needs a little magic, not even Santa may be able to stop her.

When an unexpected accident forces Hugh to confront a life-or-death decision, he may have to sacrifice everything he has worked so hard for.

And if that happens, Santa’s elves fear that Santa may never come down a chimney again.

Come with Hugh on this magical adventure about friendship, the courage to be your true self, and the amazing discovery that sometimes the greatest gifts are the ones we give from the heart.

Ask for a Blessing Box

In The Blessing Box, all Hugh wants for Christmas is a dog. But an out-of-sorts Santa has something else in mind. Santa's mysterious gift, the Blessing Box, challenges Hugh to work for what he wants.

Hugh doesn't like it one bit, until he realizes it may be just the thing to help him prove to his parents he is ready for a dog of his own. So, Hugh pet sits, mows lawns, earns his allowance, and works as hard as he can to make his dream come true.

Hugh has always known Christmas is about receiving gifts, but during his Blessing Box adventure, he learns Christmas is also about giving from the heart. Is there anything you really want for Christmas? What would you give to have it?
