Meet the Book

The Blessing Box

By Liv Fuller

Illustrated by Quincy Battieste

All Hugh wants for Christmas is a dog, but an out-of-sorts Santa has something else in mind—an idea his elves think is nuttier than a fruitcake.

When Hugh opens Santa’s unusual gift, the Blessing Box, his dream of a dog of his own crumbles like a Christmas cookie. But Hugh soon realizes the mystical box isn’t empty after all. It holds an invisible key called opportunity—one that could help him prove to his parents he’s ready for a dog.

From one Christmas to the next, Hugh, a sixth grader on the autism spectrum, works tirelessly to make his dream come true. But bullies, betrayal, and the challenge of standing up for himself may stand in the way.

Fortunately, Hugh’s mischievous best friend is determined to help—even if she lands in the doghouse trying. And if one extraordinary elf decides Hugh needs a little magic, not even Santa may be able to stop her.

When an unexpected accident forces Hugh to confront a life-or-death decision, he may have to sacrifice everything he has worked so hard for.

And if that happens, Santa’s elves fear that Santa may never come down a chimney again.

Come alongside Hugh on this magical adventure about friendship, the courage to be your true self, and the amazing discovery that sometimes the greatest gifts are the ones we give from the heart.

Meet the Author

Liv Fuller

Born in an idyllic small town in rural Wisconsin, I still remember the smell of the barn when I helped my dad treat cows with milk fever. The warmth of the old house after spending hours wading through snow drifts taller than I was. Climbing onto the hot summer roof to pelt my three younger siblings with water balloons. Wearing Green Bay Packers apparel to the little Lutheran church on the hill on crisp autumn Sundays. Playing little league and duck hunting with the boys—and beating them. And forging the strongest, longest friendships a tomboy could ask for.

I spent my early adult years in wild, wonderful Madison, Wisconsin. Mad-town, as it is called. Forty acres surrounded by reality. A place where minds stretched big, crimes cowered small, and crowds were quaint. I earned my journalism and law degrees from the University of Wisconsin-Madison, and I’ll forever have the fierce heart and drive of a Badger.

Over the past twenty-five years, metro Atlanta has become my second home. My career as an attorney and banker has paid the bills, but it’s my family that fills my heart.

I’m married to my soul mate and best friend. I’m a mama bear to two incredible children and two equally incredible bonus children. Along with our beloved pit bull rescues, they’re the loves of my life.

For all these fifty-some years, the artist in me has begged for attention. Over the past decade, I’ve finally given her what time I could, pouring my heart into writing The Blessing Box.

The character Hugh and many of the story’s events were inspired by the childhood experiences of one of our children, who was diagnosed with autism in early college. The Blessing Box is my love letter to our child and to everyone who has ever marched to the beat of their own drum, faced life’s challenges, and refused to give up.

To me, they are the real superheroes. Giving voice to ordinary people who dare to be different is both my passion and my privilege.

Always be you,
Liv

Meet the Illustrator

Quincy Battieste

Illustrator / Designer / Cartoonist

Quincy Battieste is an illustrator, designer, and cartoonist whose childhood love of drawing grew into a lifelong passion for visual storytelling. Originally from Anchorage, Alaska, he works in both traditional and digital mediums, creating imaginative artwork inspired by the absurdities of everyday life, pop culture, film, and literature. His work blends humor, nostalgia, and a healthy dose of the unexpected.

When he isn’t drawing, he enjoys movies, music, sports, and dreaming up his next creative project. He resides in Atlanta, Georgia with his wife, Laila, daughter Abigail, and his annoyingly lovable labradoodle, Chloe.

Ask for a Blessing Box

In the story, Hugh wants a dog for Christmas. But Santa, realizing Hugh is getting older, gives him a Blessing Box instead. The audacity! How dare Santa challenge Hugh to work for what he wants?

But Hugh warms up to the idea when he realizes that if he earns and saves $500 and places it in the Blessing Box under the tree on Christmas Eve, then Santa will match it, leaving Hugh with $1,000—the amount Hugh’s dad says he needs for a dog of his own—next Christmas.

If you know a child who might enjoy the Blessing Box Experience, you can request it from Santa, or it can come from you. With a little creativity, a customized Blessing Box—the challenge and reward crafted specifically for the child—could be a meaningful Christmas keepsake and presents an opportunity for ongoing mentoring from parents, godparents, grandparents, aunts, uncles, or other adults in the child’s life. After all, it takes a Christmas village.

The Blessing Box Experience includes a rigid cardboard Blessing Box, three sheets of silver paper, one sapphire-blue envelope, and one metallic-silver envelope seal. Santa embosses his seals. No one else does. The seal and who signs the letter are the only ways a child can discern whether the Blessing Box came from Santa or someone else.

Why read it?

This busy holiday season, give yourself the best gift of all—time and magical connection with the special young readers in your life.

With reminders that Jesus is the reason for the season, The Blessing Box is an uplifting Christmas adventure about courage, kindness, and generosity that reminds us to be our authentic selves and that sometimes the greatest gifts are the ones we give from the heart.

With twenty short chapters and at least one illustration per chapter, The Blessing Box is a quick and heartwarming read. Take an advent-calendar approach and read a chapter a night, or lose yourself in the book on a cozy winter afternoon. Either way, grab some hot cocoa, a blanket, and maybe a tissue or two as you snuggle up with your young readers and embark upon the Blessing Box adventure.

And who knows? Perhaps a little of the magic you discover within these pages will find its way into your own home. After all, the most meaningful Christmas messages stay with us year round. (See the Ask for a Blessing Box tab.)

FAQ

What is The Blessing Box about?

The Blessing Box follows Hugh, a sixth grader on the autism spectrum, who wants a dog for Christmas. Santa’s mysterious gift—the Blessing Box—sends Hugh on a yearlong adventure of friendship, kindness, responsibility, and discovering the gifts we give from the heart.

What genre is The Blessing Box?

Heartwarming, inspirational, Christian-based Christmas fantasy.

What age range is appropriate?

The story is ideal for young readers ages 8 to 12, the young at heart, and younger readers as a family read-along.

Does the story have a Christian perspective?

Yes. It explores kindness, courage, and giving, with reminders that Jesus is the reason for the season.

Can we read it together during the holidays?

Yes. The twenty chapters can be read one at a time or enjoyed in a longer reading session.

Are there illustrations?

Yes. The Blessing Box has at least one interior illustration per chapter, all by Quincy Battieste.
